# CodeCraft Design Brief

## Direction exploration

### 1. Midnight Lab
A focused dark workspace for making machine-readable codes, with amber actions and cyan data accents. **Probability: 0.08**

### 2. Paper & Ink Classroom
A bright notebook-like interface with paper panels, ink outlines, and primary-color controls. **Probability: 0.07**

### 3. Signal Garden
A playful educational interface with soft green, blue, and orange panels inspired by signals and patterns. **Probability: 0.06**

## Committed direction: Midnight Lab

- **Design movement:** Calm technical utility with a friendly classroom tone.
- **Core principles:** Make the input obvious, make customization discoverable, keep the generated code visually central, and always explain the current state.
- **Color philosophy:** Deep navy and slate backgrounds reduce visual noise; warm amber marks the primary action; cyan identifies data and successful output; soft mint and coral support status states.
- **Layout paradigm:** Two-column workspace on desktop, stacked flow on small screens. Controls sit in a left rail; the preview and quick tips sit in a right stage.
- **Signature elements:** A small “signal spark” logo, numbered step markers, chip-like examples, and a thin amber progress rail under the header.
- **Interaction philosophy:** Simple defaults first, advanced controls grouped by code type, immediate mode switching, and no page reloads for generation.
- **Animation:** Short fades and subtle panel lift on hover; no distracting motion.
- **Typography system:** Inter/system sans for UI clarity, monospace for values and technical labels.
- **Brand essence:** “Turn an idea into a scannable signal.”
- **Brand voice:** Encouraging, precise, and school-project friendly.
- **Wordmark/logo:** CodeCraft wordmark paired with three connected signal blocks: one amber, one cyan, one mint.
- **Signature brand color:** Amber `#f4b860`.
